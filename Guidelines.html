Importance of Safety

Cybersecurity helps protect our information, accounts, and devices. Learning basic safety habits can help people and businesses reduce online risks.

What We Protect

We protect personal information, passwords, bank details, and devices. Businesses also need to protect customer information and private company files.

What Could Happen Without Security?

Attackers could steal information, access accounts, or cause financial loss. Harmful software could damage files and interrupt work.

Why Safety Matters

Online safety helps protect our privacy and the people who depend on us. For companies, protecting information also helps maintain customer trust and keep daily work running.

How to Stay Safer

Use strong, unique passwords and turn on two-factor authentication. Keep software updated and check unexpected messages before opening links or attachments.
